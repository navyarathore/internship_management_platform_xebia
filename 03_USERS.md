**Project Name:** Internship Management Platform

**Objective:**
Design and develop a platform that connects students, recruiters, placement officers, and administrators to streamline internship applications, candidate management, and placement tracking.

**Proposed Technology Stack**

* Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
* Backend: Node.js, Express.js
* Database: MySQL
